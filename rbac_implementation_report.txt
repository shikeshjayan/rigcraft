Role Structure
--------------
1. SUPER_ADMIN - Highest privileges (config, auditing, developer, hard deletes)
2. ADMIN - General business operations
3. PRODUCT_MANAGER - Product catalog management (products, categories, brands, prebuilt PCs)
4. ORDER_MANAGER - Order operations (confirm, process, ship, deliver, cancel, return, refund)
5. SUPPORT_EXECUTIVE - Support tickets (assigned tickets only, limited customer details)
6. CUSTOMER - Standard customer access (browse, purchase, reviews, support tickets)
