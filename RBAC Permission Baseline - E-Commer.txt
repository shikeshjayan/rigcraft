RBAC Permission Baseline - E-Commerce Platform	
	
Role	Purpose
Super Admin	Full platform control, including system configuration, administrator management, role/permission management, security, and all business operations.
Administrator	Manage overall e-commerce operations including customers, catalog, orders, reviews, coupons, support, and business reports.
Product Manager	Manage the product catalog, categories, brands, pricing, specifications, compatibility, inventory, and prebuilt PCs.
Order Manager	Manage customer orders, fulfillment, shipping, cancellations, returns, refunds, and related payment/order operations.
Support Executive	Assist customers through support tickets and live chat, and access the customer/order information required to resolve issues.
Customer	Browse products, build PCs, manage cart and wishlist, place orders, make payments, submit reviews, and use customer support.
