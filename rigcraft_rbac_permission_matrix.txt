==============================================================================================================
                         ROLE-BASED ACCESS CONTROL (RBAC) PERMISSION MATRIX
==============================================================================================================

ROLES:
1. SUPER_ADMIN       : Full platform control, system config, RBAC, and auditing.
2. ADMIN             : Business operations, marketing (deals/coupons), staff oversight.
3. PRODUCT_MANAGER   : Catalog, inventory, pricing, specifications, prebuilts.
4. ORDER_MANAGER     : Order processing, fulfillment, shipping, returns, refunds.
5. SUPPORT_EXECUTIVE : Customer service, assigned tickets, live chat.
6. CUSTOMER          : End-user limited strictly to their own data.

ACCESS LEVEL DEFINITIONS:
Allow      = The role is authorized to perform the action.
Deny       = The role is not authorized.
Own        = Restricted to resources belonging to the authenticated customer.
Assigned   = Restricted to resources assigned to the staff member.
Limited    = Only information necessary for the role is available.
Global     = Applies to all permitted resources within the role's responsibility.
Public     = Available without authentication.

==============================================================================================================
                                     CORE RBAC POLICY RULES
==============================================================================================================
1. Business Logic Integration: RBAC is only the first gateway. Controllers must verify business logic 
   (e.g., "Is order refund-eligible?", "Does cart belong to user?") before actioning.
2. Escalation Control: Admins cannot create Super Admins. No role can grant permissions higher than its own.
3. Audit Trail: All Refunds, Status Changes, Role Assignments, and Blocking actions MUST log timestamp, 
   actor ID, and reason.
4. Hard Deletes: Only SUPER_ADMIN can physically destroy records from the database.
5. PII Masking: SUPPORT and ORDER staff see customer properties, but sensitive core data (password hashes, 
   gateway traces) are strictly segregated.
